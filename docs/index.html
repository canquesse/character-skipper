Character Skipper for YouTube

Automatically skip scenes of the people you choose — with private, on-device AI face recognition.

Read the privacy policy →

What it does

  • Scans a whole video and groups every face it finds, so you can pick characters visually.
  • Skips the scenes where your selected characters appear, automatically.
  • Remembers your characters across different videos.

Privacy

  • All face recognition runs locally, inside your browser.
  • Video frames are analyzed in memory and are never stored or transmitted.
  • No servers, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts.
